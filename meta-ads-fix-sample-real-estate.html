Your business, read closely

What stands out for your business

Four things matter most. Each one shapes the plan.

Your message already works

Your main ad message speaks to a real trap families feel: they can't buy until they sell, and they don't want to sell until they know where they're going. Almost 5 in 100 people who saw your ads clicked, which is in the top range for this kind of ad.

The obstacle is after the click

Your ads promise a conversation. Your page opens with a banner and a title, and the form is about three screens down on a laptop and three and a half on a phone. A visitor has to go looking for the way to talk to you.

Your warm audience is nearly used up

Your warm pool is about 2,000 to 3,000 people, and your two ads reached about 2,400 of them in 8 days. To keep growing, the pool needs to grow, which means turning your top-of-funnel campaign back on and adding people who have visited your website.

Your lead count cannot be trusted yet

Meta has recorded 4 Leads, all tests. Your thank-you page counts a lead every time it is opened, even by someone who did not fill out the form, and it is currently set to appear in search results and in your sitemap. Until that is fixed, your email inbox is the true count.

Budget and the one result to measure

The result to measure

Real form submissions. Count the Lead event in Events Manager and check it against the messages that reached your email. The number to watch is cost per submission: ad spend divided by real submissions. Visits, clicks and click rate are there to help diagnose, not to judge success.

Real estate averages $50 to $150 per lead. Under $50 is strong. Over $250 is a concern.

What this plan can and cannot do

TOF at about $5 a day plus MOF at about $4 a day is about $270 a month. That fits your $200 to $400 range. Today you spend about $105.

TOF and MOF grow your audience and bring warm people to your page, so more of them see a form that works. They are not set up to find the people most likely to fill out a form. Only a Leads campaign aims at that, and at this budget a Leads campaign will be bumpy, because Meta's system needs about 50 of those events a week to learn well. So treat a Leads campaign as the later step, once your Lead count has proven itself.
