Cost Per Result

Your ad is “doing great.” So why are you losing money?

A real comment from a beginner's ad account: their checkout cost $5. Their actual profit per sale? $4.

WHAT TO ACTUALLY DO
1 Work out your break-even cost-per-result first — what you can spend per sale and still come out ahead, given your real margin.
2 Judge every campaign against that number — not a generic “good CPA” benchmark from a completely different business.
