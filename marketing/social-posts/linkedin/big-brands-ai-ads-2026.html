✦ AI In Advertising, 2026

Coca-Cola's AI Christmas ad got called "soulless." Cadbury's AI campaign personalized ads for thousands of local shops. Same technology. Opposite reception.

✕ Backfired
Replaced the human, emotional core people already loved.
Coca-Cola's AI holiday ad — real boycott threats, two years running. Toys "R" Us — called "creepy," not magical.
AI replacing feeling, not adding speed.
✦ Landed
Used AI to do something only AI could do — at scale.
Cadbury personalized ads for thousands of local shops. H&M cut reshoot costs while keeping real models' consent.
Speed and scale. Not a personality transplant.
AI works when it's a tool applied with intention. It backfires when it's used to skip the thinking.
