07 · QuestionsStraight answers
Meta has a free AI assistant, and there are free audit tools. Why pay?
Those tools check settings and tend to recommend spending more. They don't ask whether the ads can make money at your price point, they don't look at what happens after the click, and they don't make the changes. That is what you are paying for.
I already have an agency or freelancer. Is that a problem?
No. Many people order this as a second opinion. I describe what is set up and what it is doing. I don't contact them, and what you do with the report is up to you.
Will this get me more sales?
I can't promise that, and I'd be wary of anyone who does. What you get is a clear read on what is wrong and what to fix first. Results depend on your offer, your budget and how your market responds.
My budget is small. Is this still worth it?
Yes, if ads are already running. A small budget makes waste more expensive, not less. If the budget is too small for the goal, the audit will say so.
Do we need a call?
No. Everything is in writing, in a report you can read when it suits you. Questions after delivery are answered in writing too.
Do you offer refunds?
No. Once you order, I do the work: I review your numbers, tell you what is happening, and give you proposed changes. You then decide whether to make the edits yourself or hire me to make them.
What access do you need?
Partner access through Meta Business Suite. I never ask for your password. If your Business Portfolio is brand new, access can take a few days to become available.
Does it work for my industry?
I've audited accounts for local services, real estate, e-commerce and coaching businesses. Some industries have extra Meta rules, such as housing, finance and health. If yours does, the audit covers what that means for your ads.
What if my ad account is restricted?
This audit is for accounts that can run ads. For restrictions, start with the Meta Account Clarity Review.
