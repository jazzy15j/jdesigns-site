Is it too late to start holiday ads? Not yet, but November is already late.

Black Friday is November 27, and reach costs the most that week. It costs far less right now. Five quick checks decide whether you pay early prices or peak prices for your December results.

The numbers: Reaching 1,000 people averages about $25 in Q4, 26% above the yearly average. Black Friday week runs 2-3x normal. January drops about 24% below average. These are benchmark estimates, not promises, but the pattern is clear: building your audience now is cheaper than building it in November.

1. Make sure the Meta setup is really yours

If an old agency owns your Page or Instagram, you can get locked out. Meta also now requires Business Verification for more accounts, and ads stop running without it.

Check

  • Your Business Portfolio (not an agency's) owns the Page and Instagram.
  • Business Verification is done if Meta asks for it.
  • Instagram says "Connected," not "Review Connection."

2. Make sure Meta can see your results

"The Pixel is installed" is not the same as "the Pixel is working." Bad data teaches Meta the wrong thing.

Check

  • Events Manager shows your Pixel or Conversions API as Active.
  • A test event shows up live.
  • You're optimizing for Purchase or Lead, not just PageView.

3. Get something live

Even a small awareness, traffic, or lead campaign starts Meta's learning and builds an audience you can retarget later. That costs far less than finding new people during Black Friday week. Wait only if one of these isn't true yet:

  • You have an offer people already buy.
  • Buying or booking works on a phone.
  • Meta can receive a real tracking event (see #2).
  • Your ad budget is separate from any management fee.

If all four are true and nothing is running, that's your gap. It isn't the creative or the platform.

Also get your inbox ready. Meta reports that 59% of holiday shoppers on its platforms message a business. Make sure Messenger and Instagram can handle sales questions:

  • Turn on an instant auto-reply.
  • Save replies to your top five questions (shipping, pricing, returns, availability, how to order).
  • Check your business hours and response time in Meta Business Suite.

4. Decide if Pinterest fits

Pinterest works best for visual brands: gifts, decor, food, fashion, beauty, events, travel, and crafts. By November, Pinterest says, most holiday decisions are already made, and pins need time to get found in search.

Do

  • Switch to a business account and claim your website.
  • Install the Pinterest tag.
  • Publish pins now, not in November.

5. Put dates on the calendar

Meta's advice is to start ads by mid-October and put 60-70% of your Black Friday budget in the first half of that window. Their words: "lean in, not pull back."

  • Now: finish checks 1-3 and get a campaign live.
  • Late October to early November: add retargeting once you have an audience.
  • Black Friday week: spend more early in the week. Check weekly, not daily.
  • January: reach is cheapest. Follow up with your holiday buyers, not just a discount blast.

What this checklist is — and isn't. This is a setup order backed by real, dated platform data — not a guarantee. Meta and Pinterest control ad delivery and platform behavior, and results still depend on your offer, budget, creative, and market. What a checklist like this can do is make sure nothing foundational is working against you while reach is still this cheap.
