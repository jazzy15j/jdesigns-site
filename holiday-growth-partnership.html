Why Start In August

Holiday buyers don't start deciding in November. Pinterest's own 2026 holiday guidance places campaign readiness — offer, tracking, and creative — before peak-season activation, not during it. Meta's current ad guidance points the same direction: stable campaign structure and diversified creative built ahead of time, not assembled under pressure once the season hits.

If you wait until November to start, you're not planning a holiday campaign — you're reacting to one.
