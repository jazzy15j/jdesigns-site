About MeThe short version

I run JDesigns Strategist from Nebraska — no agency, no team behind the logo, just me. I studied Advertising & Public Relations at the University of Nebraska at Kearney, then spent five years doing marketing and design work inside a local nonprofit before going out on my own in 2021. I've been a Fiverr Pro since 2024.

I'm a systems person before I'm anything else — I'd rather build a repeatable process than repeat myself. That shows up in how I run my own business as much as how I set up a client's.

My role isn't to save your business, be your tech support, or be the smartest person in the room. It's to help capable people use modern technology more effectively — organizing information, identifying bottlenecks, building repeatable workflows, and simplifying what's actually complicated.
